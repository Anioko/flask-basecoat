{# Wardley Map, C1. After Simon Wardley (Wardley Maps, 2016 to 2018): the vertical axis is visibility to the user, the user need at the top down to infrastructure; the horizontal axis is evolution in four stages, Genesis, Custom-Built, Product (+rental) and Commodity (+utility). Components are circles placed by evolution and visibility, dependencies are lines running down, movement along the evolution axis is a dashed arrow to a dashed circle, inertia is a short bar beside a component. A user (the anchor) sits at the top. Usage: {{ canvas.wardley_mapping(data) }}, data = {'title', 'subtitle', 'components': [{'id', 'name', 'kind' ('user' | 'component'), 'evolution' (0 to 1, left to right), 'visibility' (0 top to 1 bottom), 'depends_on': [ids], 'movement': {'to' (evolution), 'label', 'place'} (optional), 'inertia' (optional), 'label' (right | left | above | below), 'notes'}]}. List the components from the top of the value chain down; that is the reading and Tab order. Every component is a Button named ", evolution , visibility "; it opens a Sheet with its detail. The drawing is role="img" with the counts of components, dependencies and movements, which the list route repeats.
