{# Empathy Map, C4. Dave Gray, XPLANE (the empathy map, 2009; Gamestorming, 2010), in the four-quadrant form: the user at the centre, Says and Thinks above, Does and Feels below, with Pains and Gains as two equal halves of a strip underneath. The quadrant labels are the published ones of the Says / Thinks / Does / Feels map; the older See / Hear variant is not used. DOM order: the user first (who the map is about), then the quadrants in reading order, then Pains and Gains. Usage: {{ canvas.empathy_map(data) }}, data = {'title', 'subtitle', 'boxes': {'user': [notes], 'says': [notes], ...}}.
